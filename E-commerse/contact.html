Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

It depends on your location and the seller. You'll see updates on the Track order page as your order is confirmed and shipped.

Can I pay when the order arrives?

Yes, cash on delivery is available on eligible orders. You can also pay in advance with eSewa, Khalti or bank transfer.

Can I cancel or change my order?

You can cancel from My account while the order is still at the "Order placed" stage. After that, please contact us with your order number.

What is your return policy?

Damaged, defective or wrong items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See the Returns page for details.
